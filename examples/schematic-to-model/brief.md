# Example mechanical briefs

Use this project's STYLE contract and select `SCHEMATIC_TO_MODEL` for the task. Choose exact dimensions, tolerances, detail and finite budgets before approving one complete schematic package. Separate reference observations from assumptions and declared simplifications. The registered examples are small licensed procedural studies; they do not arrive with user approval.

## Wheeled platform

Design a simple chassis and four independently addressable wheel occurrences. Reuse a wheel definition where geometry is identical, while preserving each occurrence's identity, transform and rotary joint. Show the complete BOM, axle sections, interfaces and wheel ranges before modeling. The native `vehicle` example is deliberately minimal: it has no drivetrain, steering system, tires, commercial suspension or finished vehicle body. Its approved zero explode offsets do not demonstrate separated exploded placement. Inspect diagnostic rotation using measurable channels or a suitable authored orientation feature rather than comparing symmetric wheels in still frames.

## Opening electronic enclosure

Design an enclosure with a hinged lid, internal battery and motor shapes, two independently modeled threaded fasteners, and a separately skinned wire with named terminals and physical routing. Show the internal section, exploded parts, electrical relationships, hinge travel, screw pitch/travel and wire deformation choices together. Electrical relationships are descriptive; this is not a simulated or certified circuit. Review hidden fasteners and thin wire at useful scale in the bound diagnostic assembly, because closed exterior views cannot establish their motion quality.

## Curved vessel hull

Design an idealized curved hollow hull with a separate deck, shaft, rudder and bearing/bracket components. Preserve the hull openings and local coordinates of small parts within the larger assembly. Show longitudinal and cross-sections, nonzero exploded offsets, fixed and moving interfaces and rudder/shaft ranges. This example makes no claim about buoyancy, hydrodynamics, propulsion completeness, manufacturing or seaworthiness.

## Common deliverables and decisions

Build each definition independently, then assemble distinct physical occurrences without fusing their boundaries. Deliver editable Blender source, canonical rest GLB, drawings, BOM, assembly/mechanism metadata and provenance. Label diagnostic source and animated GLB separately as articulation-readiness evidence, not finished animation.

Review the exact complete design before construction, then technical verification, independent model review and separate human model acceptance after export. A design revision invalidates dependent approvals/evidence. MODEL_TO_ANIMATION, engine integration, gameplay, human runtime acceptance and explicit asset promotion are later operations. These briefs authorize no provider generation, installation, license acceptance or public asset use beyond applicable source rights.
