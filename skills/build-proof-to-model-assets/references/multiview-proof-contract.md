# Prepare and review one coherent conditioning set

Classify the original source set first. Select the original views that genuinely supply usable canonical coverage, then generate only missing or rejected views. Six-view `multi-view` remains the default: a single valid canonical source normally needs five additional images. Do not relabel a cropped or three-quarter painting as front; keep it as the immutable identity anchor and explicitly budget the six canonical generations it may require. Multiple originals contribute one shared coverage plan, not five generations per source.

An explicitly qualified native/host installation may additionally register `multi-view-four` with `init --mode multi-view-four --conditioning-profile PATH`. This creates a fresh schemaVersion3 manifest with `conditioningProfile: "cardinal-four-v1"` and `conditioningProfileFile`; it does not reinterpret old six-view v2 or diagnostic v1 manifests. Its schemaVersion2 proof set declares that profile and `viewCount: 4`; the original six-view proof-set v1 remains unchanged. The profile binds schemaVersion1, id, mode, MV model family, four ordered cardinal views and the accepted base-toolchain path/hash. Preserve existing installation, model/license and source evidence. Never select a profile by counting files or after a failure. Continuations read the registered immutable profile; optional `--mode` must match. Budget only missing selected slots. Four-view leaves polar coverage unknown, rather than supplying unseen polar proofs.

## Freeze shared identity and cameras

Use the exact ordered slots `front`, `right`, `back`, `left`, `top`, `bottom` for six-view; the registered four-view profile uses exactly `front`, `right`, `back`, `left`. Left/right mean the subject's sides. In the Z-up rig, front faces -Y, subject-right is -X and subject-left is +X. The right camera is therefore at -X and the left camera at +X; these are not screen-right/screen-left labels. Keep this orientation in prompts and asymmetric attachment checks. Preserve entity count, silhouette, proportions, pose, attachments, asymmetric details, material identity, palette and style across the selected set. Mirroring does not supply a second side of an asymmetric subject. Hidden surfaces are proposed completions, not newly observed facts.

The native contract uses Blender/NeRF world Z up, camera local -Z forward and +Y image up; matrices are row-major camera-to-world. Use the pipeline's fixed cardinal/polar rig, distance, horizontal FOV and positive mesh scale. Top/bottom have explicit roll; generic world-up look-at logic is singular at the poles. Keep one framing/scale convention across the set. Arbitrary illustrations do not supply measured calibration; label prescribed generated cameras `nominal-prescribed`.

Pixal3D's `meshScale` is inverse scale: its conditioning cube has half-extent `0.5 / meshScale` in camera-world units. A value of `1/6` therefore creates a six-unit cube. All prescribed cameras must sit outside that cube with a positive depth margin; positivity of the individual fields is insufficient. Verify framing against the actual padded silhouettes and camera projection before a live run. Exterior cameras alone do not establish correct calibration or visual quality. Correcting camera metadata retains image ancestry but invalidates whole-set approval and downstream results.

No per-view tight crop, stretch or silhouette enlargement is permitted during matte/input preparation. Preserve true alpha where suitable or invoke the pinned explicit BiRefNet path. Pad on a common square canvas, retain transforms and adjust horizontal FOV for centered padding. Inference receives exactly six or four distinct prepared images and matching camera JSON for its registered profile, never the contact sheet.

## Generate and revise

Resolve CLOUD/LOCAL capability, read MEDIA.md and establish rights, upload permissions and bounded set/per-view/revision budgets before generation. A proof-set selection never authorizes unlimited retries. Existing-proof reuse does not itself require generation, a new media contract or spending.

Retain each actual image, hash, original-source ancestry, classification fingerprint, camera, dimensions, generation receipt, exact prompt/references, disclosed provider/model/parameters or unavailable markers, revision lineage and rejection reasons. Use `prepare-proofs` to import and validate actual artifacts; the command neither generates images nor certifies their semantic consistency.

Target revisions to one angle and describe the exact defect. Preserve the other selected image bytes and previous candidates. Revision must retain the shared identity and fixed pose; if a structural reinterpretation is necessary, update classification from the originals and invalidate affected views. Any selected image, camera or classification change invalidates whole-set approval and dependent reconstruction evidence.

## Review before reconstruction

Show a labeled contact sheet and full-resolution originals/proofs. Inspect source likeness, entity/limb/part counts, occlusion completion, attachment continuity, fixed pose/proportions, side ownership, applicable top/bottom orientation, material identity and source-specific asymmetry. Compare the complete selected set together; plausible isolated images may still contradict one another. Mark omitted polar/occluded surfaces unknown in four-view review.

One human `proof-set` receipt may approve all six or all four exact current hashes and their classification/camera/style/profile interpretation. Use the current `status` fingerprint named `proofSet` when recording that kind. The `proof` approval kind is reserved for explicit single-view diagnostics and cannot approve a production set. Missing approvals remain pending; cross-profile receipts do not transfer. Preserve unresolved issues and rejection history. Separate preprocessing approval binds actual normalized inputs and may be recorded by a genuine technical reviewer after inspection; it does not fabricate human proof-set or final-use approval.

## Reconstruction and final review

The pinned MV engine must consume exactly the registered six or four frames and verified MV weights. Its native count can clamp to available frames, so the requested `--num-views` alone is insufficient evidence. Preserve ordered frame/camera hashes, profile hash and exact process arguments. No single-view fallback, duplicate views or omitted-slot aliases are allowed. A four-image subset cannot satisfy six-view; only its explicitly registered four-view profile accepts a complete four-image set. Four-view reuse is initially refused; never copy six-view/SV stages or approvals into it.

Blender review compares immutable originals, approved proofs, prepared conditioning images and six independent GLB renders. Six-view has six proof/render pairs; four-view has four paired cardinal comparisons plus explicitly unpaired top/bottom model-only diagnostics. It applies one declared geometry frame transform and shared fit; it does not rotate or refit the model separately per camera. Inspect actual omitted/occluded anatomy, hands, soles, attachments and riggability; nominal alignment is not measured pixel correspondence or automatic proof of likeness. Phantom transparency cannot repair malformed geometry. Keep independent review, applicable rig/animation and separate human runtime acceptance pending until explicitly recorded.

Upstream documents MV validation with four views; this does not itself qualify a host/native profile or establish an asset's quality. The six-view extension and additive four-view path require their own bounded native acceptance. Preserve old grants/history and use fresh migration versions. Structural tests, simulated processes and real Blender fixture renders must not be presented as successful live reconstruction or production quality; exact proof selection is not model acceptance or permission to spend beyond its grant.
