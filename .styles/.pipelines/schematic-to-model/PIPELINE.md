# SCHEMATIC_TO_MODEL Art Pipeline

## Intent

Create mechanically coherent objects from dimensioned schematics: vehicles, devices, vessels and other assemblies. Approve the complete design, construct every part definition independently, then assemble the physical occurrences and prove their declared articulation. Preserve the existing `PROOF_TO_MODEL` default, `STYLE.md`, engine selection and independent media domains.

Use [`build-schematic-models`](../../../skills/build-schematic-models/SKILL.md) and [`schematic-to-model.sh`](../../../commands/schematic-to-model.sh). Hephaera owns selection, rights, approvals, provenance and continuation. The exact pinned Bubo runtime owns dimensional/connectivity validation, drawings, part construction, assembly/motion evaluation and export verification. Installation, license acceptance, generated references and asset promotion require their own applicable authorization.

## Inputs

- Resolve this pipeline and read the current `STYLE.md`. A prompt override applies only to its task; it does not rewrite the contract, select an engine or activate a media provider.
- Capture purpose, dimensions, appearance/materials, internal systems, moving parts, inspection needs, detail, tolerances and finite output/process budgets. Separate reference observations, assumptions, unresolved details and declared simplifications before approval. Reference images do not establish dimensions automatically.
- Author a versioned assembly design with stable part-definition, physical-instance, subassembly, interface, joint, route and terminal identities. Preserve local frames and explicit hierarchy. Use the pinned [design contract and schema](../../../runtime/bubo/resources/schematic-to-model/README.md), [part construction contract](../../../runtime/bubo/resources/schematic-to-model/PARTS.md) and [assembly profile](../../../runtime/bubo/resources/schematic-to-model/ASSEMBLY.md). Explicit v2 design/profile is required for assembly, readiness and export; v1 preview/part approval cannot become v2 assembly approval.
- Record exact design/reference rights and separate finite process limits at initialization. Inspect configured external asset libraries before replacement work; use only copied project-local assets with rights/copy ancestry. Original libraries stay read-only. Pure procedural work needs no `MEDIA.md`; generated reference media retains its separate policy, authorization and attempt budget.

## Production Rules

1. Use `init` for a fresh task-owned asset version with explicit design, rights and process-budget records. A revision uses a new version and retained `--parent-manifest`; never overwrite approval history. Reuse a part artifact only when its definition and relevant construction inputs remain identical and verified.
2. Run `validate` and `preview`. Present the exact complete dimensioned package: orthographic views, sections through internal systems, exploded assemblies, hierarchical BOM, connection/routing diagrams, part details and assembly/flexible-rig policy sheets. Confirm inventory, dimensions, interfaces, materials, motion branches/limits and declared simplifications together.
3. Obtain actual human approval of that whole package and record `whole-assembly-design` evidence bound to its exact design/fingerprint and every artifact. A validator, individual drawing approval or synthetic QA assertion does not grant human design acceptance. A revision invalidates affected approval and dependent assembly evidence.
4. Use `doctor` to inspect actual prerequisites. Select an existing Blender 4.5 LTS explicitly for `build-parts`, `assemble`, `prove-motion`, `export` and `review`. Native production currently requires supported 64-bit Windows committed-memory enforcement. Declared capability is not executable readiness. Do not install tools, execute add-ons or fall back to another backend after failure.
5. Run `build-parts` before `assemble`. Every approved component is modeled independently; identical definitions may share geometry but every physical occurrence keeps a separate identity, object, transform and movement. Bounded extrusions, revolves, sweeps, lofts, openings and hardware/surface detail must preserve approved dimensions, functional openings and part boundaries. Only Bubo's fixed audited/hash-bound schematic Blender bundle may author its declared repository-local working copies. This batch exception grants no arbitrary script execution and does not change standalone `BLENDER` connected-authoring policy.
6. Run `prove-motion` against the current assembly. Fixed, hinge, rotary, slider and screw joints require explicit interfaces/axes/travel. Spur gears, rack-and-pinion, pulley/belt, slider-crank and planar four-bar mechanisms require supported geometry, explicit ratios and applicable branches. Reject incompatible interfaces, broken coupling, singular/invalid travel and undeclared interference. Wires, hoses, springs and belts remain separate components with actual owned skins/rigs and measured attachment, bend, stretch, compression and travel limits. Named electrical connectivity and physical routing do not imply circuit simulation.
7. Export with an explicit `--author`, then `review`. Use the pinned [export and handoff contract](../../../runtime/bubo/resources/schematic-to-model/EXPORT.md). Require raw portable verification and fresh independent re-import of rest and diagnostic GLBs. Compare actual geometry, local precision, materials, complete physical inventory, pivots, hierarchy, skin ownership/weights, channels and every declared diagnostic sample to current approved construction evidence. Metadata or counts alone do not prove a match.
8. Review six orthographic directions, two exterior angles, every internal section, an exploded view and every diagnostic sample. A central inspection cut when no section is declared is diagnostic, not an approved schematic. Record independent model review using a reviewer distinct from the author, then separate `human-model` acceptance against the exact current package, certificates, views and independent decision. `status` revalidates current evidence; preserve failures, rejected decisions and interrupted attempts rather than selecting an older success silently.

## Output Contract

The sealed non-runtime package contains editable `assembly.blend`, animation-free `assembly.glb`, the approved design, assembly/mechanism metadata, hierarchical BOM, approved drawings and provenance. The separately labeled diagnostic-motion package contains editable/portable sampled articulation and metadata. Every approved physical occurrence remains separately addressable; rigid occurrences may share a mesh, while each flexible occurrence owns its skin and rig. Preserve small local hardware precision within large assemblies and the recorded source-Z-up to glTF-Y-up metre conversion.

A current independent model decision can issue a versioned `mechanical-assembly` handoff to `MODEL_TO_ANIMATION`. Explicitly select the preserve-only profile and current descriptor through that workflow. It preserves the reviewed mixed hierarchy, component identities, pivots and per-rig ownership; it permits no new rig, retargeting or missing component. Changes to relevant design/parts/assembly/export source state invalidate the descriptor; never refresh it silently. Existing motion profiles retain their restrictions and later motion/human runtime reviews remain separate.

Diagnostic clips establish finite sampled articulation and clearance evidence, not finished production animation, arbitrary simultaneous independent-driver combinations, continuous-collision certification, manufacturing fitness, general physics or electrical behavior. Actual selected-engine integration, gameplay, asset promotion and runtime acceptance remain separate. This pipeline copies no model into a game and has no promotion operation. Automatic CAD/raster conversion and image-to-mesh reconstruction are deferred. Never switch or fall back to another pipeline silently.

## Acceptance Checks

- Validate dimensional/connectivity consistency and the exact whole-design approval before construction. Reject missing/fused components, incompatible interfaces, invalid branches/travel, undeclared interference and detached or excessively deformed flexible parts.
- Exercise vehicle, opening device and curved-hull vessel examples through actual Blender construction and export; cover every supported joint/coupling and flexible class with dedicated fixtures. Source-only readiness does not establish portable behavior.
- Independently re-import canonical rest and diagnostic exports; compare dimensions, materials, quantities, hierarchy, local precision, pivots, skin bindings and animation. Moving one repeated instance must leave unrelated occurrences unchanged.
- Report endpoint/intermediate/cycle sampling and measured clearances. Preserve complete design/technical/independent/human decisions as distinct records; a hash, success manifest, fixture assertion or single render does not establish quality acceptance.
- Test relevant source/review invalidation, semantic reproducibility, finite resource limits, cancellation, interrupted attempts, path guards and existing floor-plan/animation regressions. Preserve original ancestry and attempt history.

## Role Fit

Use `art-director`, `blender-production-artist` and `technical-3d-artist` ownership with mechanical-design and independent QA perspectives. The coordinating skill is engine-agnostic; later animation/engine owners must resolve their own compatible workflows and target prerequisites.
